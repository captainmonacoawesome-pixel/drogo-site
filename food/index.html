Food

Daily Feeding

  • Morning: One pouch of Hills Science Diet around 6am or when you are awoken against your will.
  • Evening: One pouch of what you can reach around 5pm or when he screams so much you give up.
  • Snacks: Snacks are available from the hotel menu upon incessant request. Please see your laundry cupboard for daily dry food options.

Important Notes

  • He will claim he has not been fed. This is untrue.
  • Fresh water daily. He drinks a lot but mostly it is puddle water, best to keep the bowl full.
  • Treats are a treat, NOT the right he insists they are.
